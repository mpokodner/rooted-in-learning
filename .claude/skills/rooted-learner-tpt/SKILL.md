# The Rooted Learner — TPT Listing, Search & Conversion

Sell the way a trusted teacher-colleague would recommend a resource: plain language, honest previews, one clear problem solved. Conversion comes from clarity and trust, not hype.

Quick-use instruction: For any listing → (1) title in teacher search language, (2) keyword set balancing subject/grade/context/ML-terms/format/pain, (3) the 8-part description (§4), (4) thumbnail + honest preview, (5) one CTA + relevant cross-sell. Never keyword-stuff; never claim universal fit.

## 1. Listing Workflow (concept → publish)

1. Confirm the product earns a listing (product-selection rubric, business-v2 §5).
2. Name the ONE problem it solves in teacher language.
3. Title with the formula in §2.
4. Keywords per §3.
5. Description per the 8-part structure in §4.
6. Thumbnail + preview per §5 (build with rooted-learner-design).
7. Price + bundle placement (business-v2 §6; keep price parity with website).
8. Terms of use page inside the file (individual/classroom license; → rooted-learner-legal).
9. Publish, tag category, add to relevant bundles/cross-sells.
10. Log it in the metrics tracker for later diagnosis (§8).

## 2. Title Formula

Teachers search in plain problem/format language, not brand language. Lead with what they type.

Formula: `[Grade band] [Subject/Skill] [Format] for [Learner/Context] | [Distinctive method]`

- "Grade 4-5 Reading Passages with ELD Overlays for Multilingual Learners | Low-Prep"
- "Constructed-Response Planner for English Learners (MCAP-Style, Grades 4-5)"
- NOT: "Rooted Reading Access System" (brand-only, unsearchable)

Rules: grade band early; one distinctive hook, not five; brand name is fine at the end, never instead of search terms.

## 3. Keyword Approach

Balance six buckets — pick real terms teachers use, don't stuff:

1. **Subject/skill:** reading comprehension, narrative writing, constructed response, vocabulary
2. **Grade band:** grade 4, grade 5, upper elementary
3. **Instructional context:** small group, independent reading, test prep, MTSS, centers
4. **ML/EL terms** (where genuinely appropriate): multilingual learners, English learners, ELL, ESOL, newcomers, WIDA, language scaffolds
5. **Format:** printable, digital, Google Slides, no-prep, PDF
6. **Pain point:** low prep, differentiated, scaffolded, grade-level access

Use terms only if the product truly delivers them. Repeating a phrase past natural use hurts trust and TPT ranking. If a product isn't really for newcomers, don't tag newcomers.

## 4. Product Description Structure (answer all 8, in order)

Open with a rooted-learner-brand-voice hook (a real classroom moment), then:

1. **What is it?** One plain sentence.
2. **Who is it for?** Grade band + learner + role.
3. **What problem does it solve?** The teacher pain, named.
4. **How does it work?** The routine, briefly.
5. **What's included?** Concrete list (page counts, formats).
6. **How much prep?** Honest ("print and go" only if true).
7. **What it complements / does NOT replace.** ("Layers onto your core ELA — not a replacement curriculum.")
8. **Before you buy.** Grade/standard fit, format, license note.

Close: soft CTA + related products + terms-of-use reminder.

## 5. Thumbnail & Preview Requirements

- **Thumbnail:** readable at phone size; plain topic + grade band + one focal graphic; product-family template (design §7a, §10).
- **Preview:** show real interior pages — teacher guide + a real student page. No blurred teasers of essential content. Honesty converts and reduces refunds.
- Consistent family look so the store grid reads as one system.

## 6. Bundles, Cross-Sells & CTAs

- Bundle when ≥3 related products exist; price below the sum; bundle by grade band or by problem.
- Cross-sell: end every description with 2-3 genuinely related products (the entry "try the method" product cross-sells to the core pack).
- CTA rules: one primary action ("Add the Grade 4 pack"), no fake urgency, no off-platform purchase links inside TPT.

## 7. Reviews & Post-Purchase (ethical, platform-safe)

- Include a friendly in-file note inviting honest feedback and explaining that reviews earn the buyer TPT credits — never pay for or trade reviews, never gate the resource behind a review.
- Post-purchase: a quick-start page inside the file (how to use it tomorrow) + an invitation to the free resource/email list (allowed inside your own product; don't direct TPT buyers off-platform to purchase).
- Respond to reviews graciously; fix real issues fast and update the file.

## 8. Diagnostic Checklist for Underperformers

Isolate the funnel stage:

- **Low impressions** → search problem: weak/wrong keywords, title lacks searched terms, thin category tags. Fix title + keywords (§2-§3).
- **Impressions but low clicks** → thumbnail/title problem: tile unreadable, unclear topic/grade, off-brand family look. Fix thumbnail (§5).
- **Clicks but low conversion** → description/preview/price problem: unclear what's included, prep unclear, preview hides the goods, price mismatched. Fix the 8-part description + preview + price.

Also check: honest fit (are returns/low ratings signalling overclaiming?), and whether the product is really solving one urgent problem.

## 10. Three Worked Examples (illustrative concepts, not confirmed products)

**Example A — Entry "try the method" ($6-9)**
- Title: "Grade 4-5 Reading Passage with ELD Overlays | One Text, Three Language Supports"
- Hook: "You know the passage is right for your class. You also know two of your newcomers can't get in the door yet."
- Solves: one rigorous passage made accessible at three language levels, no rewrite.
- Prep: print and go. Complements: your core ELA block.

**Example B — Core pack ($18-28)**
- Title: "Grade 5 MCAP-Style Reading + ELD Overlay Pack | 6 Passages, Low-Prep for Multilingual Learners"
- Included: 6 original passages, selected/constructed-response items, three-tier language supports, teacher notes.
- Before you buy: aligned to grade-5 reading standards; original passages (no released test items).

**Example C — Grade-band bundle**
- Title: "Grades 4-5 ELD Reading Bundle | Passages + Overlays + Constructed-Response Planner"
- Bundle logic: entry + two core packs + planner, priced below the sum; bundled by grade band.

## 11. "Do Not" Rules

- Don't keyword-stuff or repeat phrases unnaturally.
- Don't claim a product fits every learner, grade, state, curriculum, or ML profile.
- Don't hide essential content behind blurred previews.
- Don't undercut TPT pricing on the website; don't put off-platform purchase links inside TPT.
- Don't reproduce released MCAP passages/items or any copyrighted curriculum (→ rooted-learner-legal); products must be original or licensed.
- Don't trade, buy, or gate reviews.
